PAPER PRESENTATION

Teams: 1–3 members.

Present a research or technical paper.

Explain the topic clearly and confidently.

Judges evaluate knowledge and presentation.

Focuses on research and communication skills.

Best presentation wins.

PROJECT PRESENTATION

PROJECT PRESENTATION

Teams: 1–3 members.

Present Software or Hardware projects.

Projects are evaluated by a panel.

Tests innovation and technical knowledge.

Presentation and explanation skills are assessed.

Best project wins.

SEEK & SCAN

SEEK & SCAN

Teams scan unique QR codes.

Each QR contains 5 questions.

Solving them gives a clue to the next QR where placed.

Teams enter a code to unlock the next round.

The challenge has 3 rounds.

Fastest team on the leaderboard wins.

CODING RELAY

CODING RELAY

Teams: 1–3 members.

Three levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.

Easy: 1 member solves.

Medium: 2 members solve.

Hard: 3 members solve.

Fastest team on the leaderboard wins.

NON-TECHNICAL EVENTS

PICK THE PICTURE

PICK THE PICTURE

Pictures from movies and songs are displayed.

Teams identify the picture or related content.

Some challenges involve singing or performing.

Tests memory and entertainment knowledge.

Quick and accurate answers earn points.

Highest-scoring team wins.

ACTION ECHO

ACTION ECHO

One member from each team forms a line.

An action is shown to the last person.

The action is passed from person to person.

Each participant must memorize and repeat it.

The first person performs the final action.

Most accurate team wins.
